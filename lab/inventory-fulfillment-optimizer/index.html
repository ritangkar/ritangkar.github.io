Fulfillment Optimizer

Chooses the cheapest SLA-aware fulfilment plan across a warehouse network, splitting shipments when needed.

The problem

Where an order ships from trades off cost, speed and stock. A cheap plan that misses the SLA is not cheap.

How it works

  1. Compute Haversine distance to each warehouse.
  2. Cost = ₹50 + ₹0.05/km + ₹8/kg; days = 1 + ceil(km / 500); add a ₹100/day penalty for lateness.
  3. Prefer the best single warehouse with full stock; otherwise split each item to the nearest warehouse that has it.

Technically interesting

A late delivery is priced into the score, so the optimiser can knowingly choose a plan that misses the SLA when every alternative is worse.

Why it matters

Order-management decisions are cost/SLA trade-offs; making the trade-off explicit is the core of fulfilment optimisation.

Scope & honesty. A greedy strategy over synthetic data. Warehouse capacity is modelled but not enforced.
