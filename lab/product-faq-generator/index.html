FAQ Generator

INTERACTIVE DEMOhard-coded · synthetic data · runs in your browser

The Problem

Generated support content that invents specifications is a liability.

How It Works

  1. Emit colour, feature, spec, use-case and audience FAQs only when the source field exists — remove the field and the question disappears.
  2. Check answers for unit claims and colours that are absent from the product data.
  3. Run quality checks: well-formed, non-duplicate, non-trivial.

Technically Interesting

The generator never guesses a missing field; the unsupported-answer detector is the part that would police an LLM.

Why It Matters

It demonstrates the guardrail pattern for grounded generation.

Scope & honesty. Template-driven; no LLM in the demo.
