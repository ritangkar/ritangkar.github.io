SEO Description Generator

A staged content pipeline that generates SEO copy and checks it against the product facts.

INTERACTIVE DEMOhard-coded · synthetic data · runs in your browser

The Problem

Scaling product copy risks both weak SEO and made-up claims.

How It Works

  1. Extract facts and analyse keywords.
  2. Generate title, meta, descriptions, bullets, slug and FAQs in a chosen tone.
  3. Validate SEO rules and run a factuality check that any number-plus-unit claim exists in the source data.

Technically Interesting

Quality is scored deterministically: SEO violations and factuality issues each reduce the score.

Why It Matters

Shows where the guardrails sit in a generation pipeline, independent of which model writes the text.

Scope & honesty. Template-based generation in the demo; no LLM.
