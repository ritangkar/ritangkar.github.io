Architecture

How I think about commerce systems

Technology choices follow the business question. Each layer below earns its place by changing an outcome — and each one has evidence in the Lab.

  1. 01Business

    Start from the revenue, cost or service problem — not the platform.

  2. 02Commerce

    B2B, B2C and B2B2C models on SAP Commerce, kept close to out-of-the-box capability.

  3. 03Architecture

    Data models and boundaries that stay maintainable as customisation grows.

  4. 04Integration

    S/4HANA, Service Cloud, CPI, logistics and messaging as designed, supportable flows.

  5. 05Performance

    Measure first: indexing, caching, pagination, query shape. See the benchmarks.

  6. 06Security

    Authorisation at the right layer, every time. See the model.

  7. 07Automation

    Rules and decision engines with visible thresholds. See an example.

  8. 08AI

    Grounded, validated and explainable — with a human able to override.
